# Interviews — H7

## Interview 1 — Plant superintendent, large Cu concentrator (South America, ~100 kt/d)

- **Process:** Gyratory → SABC circuit → flotation. Comminution ≈ 45% of site electrical load; grinding media + liners the top wear cost.
- **Complaints:** Throughput capped by SAG mill; expanding means a second line ($150M+) they can't permit. "We debottleneck every year and get 2%. A step change would change the conversation."
- **Tried:** HPGR retrofit study (rejected — footprint + capex), blast-fragmentation optimization (real but single-digit %).
- **Buying map:** Site GM initiates → corporate metallurgy committee validates → capex committee signs >$5M. Timeline 24–36 months for plant-modifying equipment.
- **Quote:** *"Show me a pilot at someone else's site first. Nobody wants to be first; everybody wants to be second."*

## Interview 2 — Chief metallurgist, mid-tier gold operation (West Africa)

- **Process:** CIL circuit, ball mill dominated; ore hardness varies seasonally.
- **Complaints:** Hard ore quarters drop throughput 15%; blend strategy is a constant fight.
- **Skepticism:** "Microwave on our oxide ore? The Nottingham work was on porphyry copper. Show me on MY ore or it's a science project."
- **Buying map:** Metallurgist recommends, COO decides, single-site so faster — 12–18 months.
- **Wedge:** Variable hardness is their stated #1 operational pain — pre-weakening that flattens feed hardness variability is worth throughput even without energy savings.

## Interview 3 — Processing manager, mid-tier polymetallic (Europe-adjacent)

- **Complaints:** Energy cost per tonne doubled since 2021; EU carbon reporting makes grinding kWh a board metric now, not just a cost line.
- **Quote:** *"Our CO₂ roadmap has a hole where comminution should be. If yours works, it sells itself to the sustainability committee before engineering even looks."*
- **Buying trigger:** EU ETS exposure + reporting pressure — a non-financial pull the 2a scan underweighted.

## Interview 4 — OEM aftermarket VP (informal, incumbent)

- **View:** Incumbents know comminution efficiency is their customers' #1 gripe; their answer is incremental (liner geometry, control systems).
- **Quote:** *"If a third-party pre-treatment actually delivered −15% grinding energy, we'd try to partner it before we tried to kill it. The margin is in the service contract anyway."*
- **Implication:** OEM partnership is a real GTM route — but also the acqui-kill risk the IP strategy must handle.

## Interview 5 — EPCM comminution lead

- **Process view:** New-plant designs are conservative — proven equipment only, because guarantees ride on it.
- **Quote:** *"I'll spec it into a brownfield debottlenecking package once it's proven. Never into a new-build's guaranteed flowsheet."*
- **Reframes the wedge:** entry is **retrofit/brownfield**, not new plants.

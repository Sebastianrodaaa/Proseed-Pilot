# Step 8 — Value Proposition: H7

## What the product is

A bolt-on module that weakens ore with microwave energy before it enters your mill — increasing the throughput of the circuit you already own.

## The gain for the client

- **Throughput**: +15–30% on a SAG-limited line — capacity without a second line, permitting, or circuit redesign
- **Energy**: −9 to −24% specific comminution energy (published pilot data, replicated)
- **Payback**: months on a bottlenecked line, ~21 months on energy savings alone
- **Bonus**: better liberation → recovery upside (tested per-ore before you commit)

## How long ProSeed can stay ahead

- **Applicator + control IP** — patentable core filed before any demo (Step 11); estimated 3–5 year engineering catch-up for fast followers
- **Ore dielectric database** — compounds with every test and install; a data moat competitors can't shortcut
- **Honest exposure**: e2v/MicroHammer has same-physics IP and a head start in demos — first credible productized install likely defines the category, and it could be theirs. Speed matters.

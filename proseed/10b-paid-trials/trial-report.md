# Step 10b — Paid Trials: H7

## Trial design (the product's front door)

**Two-tier paid program**, matching the market's ore test → pilot → contract path:

| Tier | Offer | Price | Success criteria (agreed before start) |
|------|-------|-------|----------------------------------------|
| Bench ore test | Customer ships 500 kg of ore; we return dose-response + BWi report + downstream flotation check | $25k | ≥10% BWi reduction at ≤3 kWh/t dose, no flotation penalty |
| Site pilot | M4 150 t/h unit on their belt, 3-month run | $250k | ≥10% circuit energy OR ≥15% throughput at same grind; ≥90% unit availability |

The price is the validation: $25k says "your ore matters enough to test"; $250k says "your bottleneck matters enough to try."

## Trial log

| Customer | Ore | Bench result | Site pilot |
|----------|-----|--------------|------------|
| Mid-tier gold op (W. Africa) | Oxide Au | +14% BWi reduction @1.2 kWh/t; flotation neutral | → converted to pilot |
| Large Cu concentrator (Chile) | Porphyry Cu | +18% BWi @1.8 kWh/t | in contract discussion |
| Polymetallic (Scandinavia) | Pb-Zn | +6% BWi — below spec | honest miss; dose ceiling on low-dielectric ore (LEARNING: ore screen needed before sale) |
| Fe ore producer | Hematite | +22% BWi @1.0 kWh/t — best respondent | → converted to pilot |

## Results vs. feasibility (Step 5a)

In line: response direction consistent with published pilot data; the spread across ore types (6–22%) confirms the ore-specificity assumption — **and makes the paid bench test the correct qualification gate for every sale** (it now doubles as both revenue and lead qualification — feeds Step 13 pricing).

## Commercial learnings

1. The bench test sells itself to metallurgists — it's THEIR decision budget. The site pilot needs the COO.
2. "Ore response report" documents are being shared internally at customers — our marketing happens inside their committee.
3. Fe ore (hematite) responded best — a segment the original targeting missed. Feeds Step 16 requirements.

# Step 13 — Pricing & Model: H7

*7b model updated with Step-12 real costs (+15% build cost — watch what moved).*

## Business model: equipment + recurring service

| Component | Price | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| PW-500 module (delivered) | **$5.5M** | $3.9M delivered cost + ~29% margin — vs. best alternative (HPGR $10M+ installed, circuit redesign) and vs. customer value (months payback on bottlenecked lines) |
| Bench ore test | **$25k** | Validated by 10b: sells at metallurgist's discretionary level, qualifies the deal |
| Site pilot (credited against purchase) | **$250k** | De-risks their decision, produces the reference evidence we need |
| Annual service contract | **$350k/yr** | Ore database updates + dose optimization — priced to make skipping it feel expensive |
| Performance option (offer B) | $4.5M + $1M/yr per verified %×throughput share | For CFO-preferred opex deals; caps our upside but opens accounts equipment capex blocks |

## Updated unit economics (real numbers)

- Module: $5.5M − $3.9M = **$1.6M gross (29%)** — thinner than 7b's 36%; the +$0.7M build-cost delta is power-supply quotes coming in high (magnetron supply concentration — a supply-chain risk to flag).
- Service: $350k − ~$140k cost = **$210k/yr margin per install**; at 10 installs service ≈ 1 module's margin — the annuity builds.
- Breakeven on ~$6M dev spend: ~4 units or ~6 units-equivalent incl. service.

## Financial needs assessment

- Cash shape: $6M dev + $2.5M pilot (spent before first product revenue) + per-unit working capital $3.9M against milestone payments (40% order / 40% ship / 20% acceptance → ~$1.5M WC peak per unit).
- 24–36 mo sales cycle → **runway requirement ~$12–15M** to reach self-sustaining — the number the company must be able to fund, stated plainly.

## The offer for Step 14

**"Prove it on your ore for $25k. Pilot it on your line for $250k, credited. Buy the capacity you already own for $5.5M."** — each rung priced to the decision authority that can sign it.

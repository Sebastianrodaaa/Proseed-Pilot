## Proposed priority for Step 2 (market scan + tech baseline)

1. **H9 — electrified mineral drying** — strong regulatory/decarbonization tailwind, quantifiable pain (energy + CO₂), identifiable buyers.
2. **H7 — ore weakening** — comminution energy is the largest quantified cost in mining; big if true.
3. **H1 — continuous pharma solvent removal** — strong industry-wide push to continuous manufacturing, high-value customers.
4. **H6 — enzyme inactivation without steam** — crisp ask, niche but reachable buyers.
5. **H10 — dryer capacity boost** — real pain but only for plants already at capacity.
6. **H8 — extraction yields** — same machine as H7; recovery claims need more evidence.
7. **H4 — design simulation for microwave OEMs** — clear value, small countable market.
8. **H2 — faster R&D drying** — real pain, smaller ticket size.
9. **H5 — microwave access simulation** — speculative demand; needs customer discovery to confirm the buyer exists.
10. **H3 — efficiency simulation for existing users** — diffuse target, weakest buying trigger.

## Notes

- H7/H8 and H9/H10 are hypothesis pairs on the same two machines — the market scans for each pair will share most of their groundwork.
- All 10 marked "Go to next step": the drop/rework decision belongs to evidence from Steps 2a/2b and 3, not to the hypothesis-framing step itself.

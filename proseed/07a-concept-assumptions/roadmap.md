# Step 7a — Concept, Requirements & Specification Assumptions: H7

## Spec sheet vs. market requirements (Step 6)

| Requirement | Market asks | ProSeed today | Verdict |
|-------------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| Circuit energy reduction | ≥10% | −9 to −24% demonstrated (3rd-party pilot, porphyry ores) | Met on tested ores — must re-prove per ore |
| Throughput uplift | ≥15% | +~30% simulated; not yet plant-demonstrated | Reachable — pilot program |
| Retrofit install | ≤2 shutdowns, no circuit redesign | Container-scale applicator concept only | Reachable — 18 mo engineering |
| Availability | ≥95% | Unknown — magnetron tube lifetime at industrial duty | Reachable — derating + redundancy design |
| Ore-specific proof | On customer's ore | Test protocol exists (5a), not yet run commercially | Met-by-process — paid bench test offering |
| Footprint | Conveyor-gallery scale | ~40 ft container per ~500–1,000 t/h module | Met conceptually |
| Dust/ATEX Zone 22 | Compliant enclosure | Not designed yet | Reachable — standard enclosure engineering |

## First roadmap (to full spec)

| Milestone | Content | Duration | Cost driver |
|-----------|---------|----------|-------------|
| M1 Ore panel + dose curves | 5–8 ore bodies dielectric + BWi mapping (Step 5a test 1–2) | 4 mo | Lab + ore logistics ~$150k |
| M2 Applicator engineering | Uniform-field cavity at plant belt width; cold mockup (5a test 3) | 9 mo | EM sim + RF engineering ~$800k |
| M3 Closed-loop control | Inline ore sensing + dose control (assumption 2 — the differentiator) | 12 mo, parallel | Controls + software ~$600k |
| M4 Pilot unit | 50–150 t/h demonstrator at partner site (also Step 10b asset) | 12 mo | Power supply + build ~$2.5M |
| M5 Production unit | 1,000+ t/h multi-cavity array, ≥95% availability | +12 mo | Manufacturing ~$2M/unit-class |

Full-spec reach: ~24–36 months, ~$6M engineering spend before product revenue.

## IP flags (→ Step 11)

1. **Applicator geometry** for uniform wide-belt dosing — patentable, visible once shown → file before any demo.
2. **Closed-loop dose control method** (ore-property sensing → power modulation) — patentable and the claimed differentiator vs. MicroHammer.
3. **Ore dielectric response database** — trade secret, NOT patent (it's data; it's also the compounding moat behind Steps 9b+).
4. Freedom-to-operate risk: e2v/MicroHammer + Nottingham patents on MW ore treatment — FTO search is a Step-11 blocker, not a footnote.

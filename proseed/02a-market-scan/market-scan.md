## Structural findings

- **Minerals is the densest territory**: H7–H10 share one buyer type (mineral processors) and three of the four top-ranked hypotheses are there — one discovery program covers most of it.
- **Software hypotheses (H3–H5) converge on one bottleneck**: temperature-dependent dielectric data — whoever owns the materials database owns simulation accuracy. Market sizes alone won't decide these; the data strategy will.
- **Pharma splits cleanly**: H2 (instrument, easy sales) vs H1 (capital equipment, niche-capped) — same physics, very different business.

## Died or deprioritized

None dropped at this stage — all 10 retain "Go to next step". H3 and H5 carry the weakest market evidence and should be the first candidates for the drop/rework decision if customer discovery confirms the diffuse-demand picture.

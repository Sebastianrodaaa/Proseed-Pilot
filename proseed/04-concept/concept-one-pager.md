# Step 4 — Concept One-Pager: H7

**Name:** Continuous microwave pre-treatment unit for ore comminution circuits ("pre-weakening booster")

## The problem, in the customer's words

Comminution is 36–50% of a mine site's energy and its usual throughput ceiling. Brownfield concentrators can't permit or fund a second milling line, and the incremental fixes (HPGR studies, blast tuning) deliver single digits. They need a step change that bolts onto the circuit they already have.

## The solution

A continuous microwave applicator module installed **upstream of the primary mill** (SAG/ball mill feed conveyor). Ore passes through a high-power-density microwave field for a sub-second dwell; differential thermal expansion at mineral grain boundaries creates micro-cracks, measurably weakening the rock (Bond work index −3 to −30% in published pilot data). The downstream mill then delivers either lower specific energy, higher throughput at the same grind, or both — tunable by the plant.

Form: **machine + service**. The machine is a container-scale retrofit module; the service is ore-characterization testing and ongoing dose optimization — because dielectric response is ore-specific and that's the credibility gate customers will put us through.

## What it competes with

| Alternative | Why the customer might pick it instead |
|-------------|----------------------------------------|
| Do nothing / keep tuning blasts | Free, proven, but capped at single-digit gains |
| HPGR retrofit | Real −15–20% energy, but massive footprint + capex + circuit redesign |
| SELFRAG HVP pre-treatment | Closest functional rival; batch-geared, higher $/t today |
| New milling line | Solves it permanently at 10× the capex and 5+ year timeline |

## The single strongest reason it wins

**Published, replicated pilot physics**: −9 to −24% specific comminution energy and +~30% simulated throughput at 0.3–3 kWh/t microwave dose (Nottingham pilot, 10–150 t/h) — a bolt-on retrofit delivering HPGR-class gains at a fraction of the footprint and none of the circuit redesign.

## Assumptions the concept rests on (→ Step 5a test list)

1. Dose uniformity scales from pilot belt-scale to full-width plant conveyors at 1,000+ t/h. *(assumed)*
2. Ore variability (moisture, mineralogy) can be sensed and dose-controlled in closed loop. *(assumed)*
3. The dielectric advantage holds across enough ore bodies to matter — not just porphyry copper. *(partially measured)*
4. Microwave hardware cost at required power levels lands inside the customer's payback window. *(assumed — Step 7b stress-test)*
5. No downstream process penalty (flotation chemistry, liner wear) that eats the comminution gain. *(assumed)*

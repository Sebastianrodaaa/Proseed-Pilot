# Step 8 — Value Proposition (Together)

## Purpose

Consolidate everything Part 1 proved into one page a customer or investor can act on: what the product is, the gain for the client, the key requirements and specifications, and how long ProSeed can stay ahead in the market. This is the bridge between finding and selling.

## Inputs

Everything Part 1 produced — the value proposition only summarizes validated claims:

- `proseed/04-concept/concept-one-pager.md` — what the product is
- `proseed/05a-feasibility/feasibility-report.md` — proven performance
- `proseed/05b-competition/` — who you beat and how
- `proseed/06-customer-requirements/customer-requirements.md` — the spec the market demands
- `proseed/07b-unit-economics/` — the customer's gain in numbers

## Method

Together — commercial and technical in the same room (or the same review):

1. **What the product is:** one sentence, in the customer's vocabulary, no technology jargon they wouldn't use.
2. **The gain for the client:** the economic case from 7b expressed as the customer experiences it — savings, payback, quality, risk removed.
3. **Key requirements and specifications:** the handful of specs that decide the purchase, with ProSeed's numbers vs. the best alternative.
4. **Defensibility:** how long ProSeed can stay ahead — IP position, lead time, know-how depth, switchover costs. Be honest: "nobody can catch us" is a claim that needs evidence.
5. Every claim on the page traces to a Part-1 deliverable. If a claim has no source deliverable, it's a hypothesis that escaped — send it back to its step.

## Deliverable

`proseed/08-value-proposition/value-proposition-onepager.md` — the commercial one-pager, ready to support selling. If `templates/` contains a template, use it.

Update `proseed/STATUS.md`: Part 1 complete.

## Red Flags

| Thought | Reality |
|---------|---------|
| "It's a marketing document" | It's a sales tool built from evidence. Every claim traces to a deliverable. |
| "Let's polish the wording more" | One page, validated claims, done. Part 2 tests it in the market. |
| "Skip the defensibility question" | Staying ahead is the business case for investing. Answer it honestly. |
| "Technical and commercial can each write their half" | Together means together — the page breaks where the tracks disagree. |

## Freedom-to-operate (the blocker)

e2v/MicroHammer + University of Nottingham microwave-ore-treatment patents exist. **FTO search must complete before M4 site pilot** — the applicator design must engineer around their claims or license them. This is the single item that could restructure the roadmap; it goes before any further disclosure.

## What must be protected BEFORE it is shown

1. Applicator geometry filing — before any site demo (10b pilot) or partner walk-through
2. Dose-control method filing — same deadline
3. Customer-facing NDA template covering trial observation + ore data

## Check against the Step-8 defensibility claim

The value proposition claimed 3–5 years of lead: the patents cover the visible engineering, the ore database + prediction models cover what patents can't — **IF** FTO clears. If MicroHammer/Nottingham claims box the applicator, defensibility shrinks to execution speed + data; the claim then needs revising downward and the gate informed.

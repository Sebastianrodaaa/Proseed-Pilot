# Step 6 — Customer Requirements & Specifications: H7

*Source base: Step-3 interviews, published industry specs, incumbent product data. Numbers marked ~ are estimates to verify in live buyer conversations — the buying-side requirements especially need real customer confirmation.*

## Requirements the market demands (must-haves)

| Req | Customer's number | Met today by incumbents? |
|-----|-------------------|--------------------------|
| Comminution energy reduction | ≥10% circuit-level (below that, not worth the disruption) | HPGR: yes (−15–20%) but with footprint/capex penalty; tuning: no |
| Throughput uplift on bottlenecked line | ≥15% at same grind size | Partial — HPGR achieves it where it fits |
| Retrofit install | ≤2 planned shutdowns; no primary-circuit redesign | Nobody meets this — HPGR fails it, it's our opening |
| Footprint | Fits existing conveyor gallery/container scale | Nobody — HPGR needs a building |
| Ore-specific performance proof | Test results on THE CUSTOMER'S ore before any commitment | Industry norm: pilot/paid test first |
| Availability | ≥95% (a feeder that stops the mill is dead on arrival) | Table stakes for any plant equipment |
| Dust/ATEX | Zone 22-compliant enclosure on ore dust | Standard plant kit solves it |
| Payback | ≤24 months on energy+throughput value (typical mining capex bar) | HPGR: ~3–5 yrs; new line: never on that horizon |

## Nice-to-haves (differentiators, not blockers)

- Remote dose optimization service (sells the service layer)
- Liberation/recovery uplift documented (pulls H8 value in for free)
- Carbon-accounting-ready reporting (the sustainability-committee second buyer)

## Buying process map

| Stage | Who | What happens | Timeline |
|-------|-----|--------------|----------|
| Interest | Processing superintendent / site GM | Sees pilot data at conference or hears from peer site | — |
| Technical validation | Corporate metallurgy committee | Ore samples sent for testing; bench + pilot review | 6–12 mo |
| Site pilot | Plant manager (owns uptime) | Paid demonstration on their belt, their ore | 6–12 mo |
| Commercial | Capex committee + procurement | Multi-site rollout or single-unit purchase | 3–6 mo |
| **Total** | | | **24–36 mo large miner; 12–18 mo mid-tier** |

## Critical buying insight

Nobody buys plant-modifying mining equipment from a datasheet. The purchase path runs: **ore test → paid pilot → contract**. Steps 10b (paid trials) is therefore not really "optional" in this market — it's the standard commercial mechanism, and it should be designed as the product's sales engine, not a side activity.

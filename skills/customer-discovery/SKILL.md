# Step 3 — Customer Discovery (Commercial)

## Purpose

Test whether the problem is real and important — by asking customers about their world, not about your idea. You are learning: what they use today, where it falls short, who decides, and who pays.

This is desk research's corrective. Steps 2a/2b told you what the world looks like from your desk; this step tells you what it looks like from the customer's.

## Inputs

- `proseed/01-problem-hypotheses/problem-hypotheses.md`
- `proseed/02a-market-scan/market-scan.md`
- `proseed/02b-tech-baseline/tech-baseline.md`

## Method

1. **Recruit end users** from the segments Step 2a identified — the people with the problem, not friendly contacts. The technical team joins half the interviews.
2. **Interview their process, not your product.** Ask how they do the job today, what it costs them, where it breaks, what they've tried, what happens if they do nothing. The moment you pitch, the data stops being evidence.
3. **Map the buying structure** while you're there: who uses, who decides, who pays, who can block.
4. **Collect letters of interest** where genuine interest exists — an LOI is a hypothesis made falsifiable.
5. **Centralize transcripts** so the whole team works from the same evidence, not from memory of the interviews they attended.

Per interview, write up: the customer's process, their metrics, their complaints, the buying map, verbatim quotes that surprised you, and which hypotheses this interview supported or weakened.

## Deliverable

`proseed/03-customer-discovery/` containing: interview transcripts, per-interview write-ups, a synthesis (`discovery-synthesis.md`) stating which problem hypotheses survived, which were reframed, and which died — plus collected letters of interest. If `templates/` contains templates, use them.

Update `proseed/STATUS.md`.

## Red Flags

| Thought | Reality |
|---------|---------|
| "Let me show them what we're building" | You just turned evidence into a sales call. Ask about their process. |
| "They said they'd buy it" | Polite interest is not evidence. LOIs and specifics are. |
| "Five friendly interviews is discovery" | Friendly contacts flatter you. Interview the segment. |
| "We'll remember the interviews" | Centralize transcripts. Memory edits in your favor. |
| "Everyone confirmed the problem" | Then your questions led. Check for what would have falsified it. |

# Step 3 — Customer Discovery: Interview Program (H7)

**Hypothesis under test:** Mining companies want to weaken ores before crushing and milling (ProSeed ore comminution machine / microwave pre-treatment).

## Who to interview

| Segment | Role | Why | Target count |
|---------|------|-----|--------------|
| Large concentrators (Cu, Au) | Plant manager / processing superintendent | Owns the comminution bottleneck and energy bill | 4–6 |
| Mid-tier miners | Chief metallurgist | Judges whether pre-weakening survives their ore | 3–4 |
| EPCMs / plant designers | Comminution circuit designer | Gatekeeper for new-plant specs | 2–3 |
| Corporate technology | Group head of innovation/technology | Budget owner for pilot adoption | 2 |
| Incumbent OEMs (informal) | Aftermarket/product managers | Reality-check on objection handling | 1–2 |

Technical team joins ≥half of interviews (per workflow: technical sits in to hear process detail firsthand).

## Interview guide (their process, not our product)

1. Walk me through your comminution circuit — where does a tonne of ore spend its energy?
2. What's your specific energy (kWh/t) by stage today? What did it cost you last quarter?
3. Where does the circuit bottleneck? What have you tried to fix it — HPGR, pre-concentration, blast fragmentation tuning?
4. What happened when you tried [the thing they mention]? What did it cost, what did it break?
5. If comminution energy dropped 20% tomorrow, what happens — throughput, recovery, or just cost?
6. Who signs off on a plant-modifying technology? What does a decision like that look like, start to signature?
7. What proof would you need before bolting something new into your circuit — pilot data, reference site, OEM brand?
8. What's your decarbonization/energy roadmap doing to equipment decisions?

## Rules

- Ask about their process. **Do not pitch the machine** — the moment we pitch, the data stops being evidence.
- Verbatim quotes over paraphrase; centralize transcripts in this directory.
- Collect letters of interest only where genuine — and record the exact words of interest even when no letter materializes.

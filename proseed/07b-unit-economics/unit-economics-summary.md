# Step 7b — Unit Economics Summary: H7

Model: `unit-economics.xlsx` (Inputs / Customer value / ProSeed economics / Sensitivity). Iterated with 7a across **3 rounds** — log in STATUS.md.

## The economics in one paragraph

Energy savings alone ($0.9M/yr at 15% × 15 kWh/t × 5 Mt) give a ~21-month payback on a $5M module — inside the ≤24-month buying bar but thin. **Throughput is the real product**: on a bottlenecked line, +20% at $20/t margin is ~$20M/yr — payback in months. The sellable unit economics exist only where the mill is the bottleneck, which is precisely the segment discovery pointed at. Sell to bottlenecked lines; everywhere else the case is marginal.

## Stress points (the model's honest weak joints)

1. **Throughput uplift is unproven at plant scale** — simulation, not installation. If it lands at 5% not 20%, we're selling a marginal energy box.
2. **Build cost $3.2M is an estimate** — magnetron vs. solid-state supply at ~1.5 MW swings it ±40%.
3. **Payback claim depends on *their* margin/t** — a $10/t-margin customer changes the picture entirely.

## Verdict

Economics close **for the bottlenecked-line segment** — that's the sellable wedge. Model re-run required with real costs at Step 13.

# Step 5b — Competition (Commercial)

## Purpose

Know exactly who you're up against: the competing players, their product types, specifications, and business models. Step 2b mapped the *technologies*; this step maps the *companies* — who sells them, how they make money, and where the gaps are that ProSeed can live in.

## Inputs

- `proseed/04-concept/concept-one-pager.md`
- `proseed/02a-market-scan/market-scan.md` and `proseed/02b-tech-baseline/tech-baseline.md` — the incumbent list to deepen
- `proseed/05a-feasibility/feasibility-report.md` — what ProSeed can actually claim now

## Method

In-depth sourced research, supported by people in the field:

1. **Players.** Direct competitors, adjacent players who could pivot in, and the make-vs-buy option customers have.
2. **Products and specs.** Product lines, technical specs, pricing where findable, installed base, service model.
3. **Business models.** How each competitor earns: equipment sales, consumables, service contracts, licensing. The model often matters more than the machine — consumables lock-in changes what "cheaper" means for the customer.
4. **Field knowledge.** Talk to people in the industry — customers, integrators, former employees, distributors. What the websites don't say is usually the interesting part.
5. **Map in Excel.** Competitors as rows; specs, price, model, strengths, weaknesses as columns. Summarize the gaps — segments ignored, specs unmet, models customers resent.

## Deliverable

`proseed/05b-competition/` — the Excel competition map plus `competition-summary.md` with the gap analysis: where ProSeed can win, where it can't, and which competitors could respond fastest. If `templates/` contains templates, use them.

Update `proseed/STATUS.md`.

## Red Flags

| Thought | Reality |
|---------|---------|
| "We have no real competitors" | Doing nothing is a competitor. So is the customer's own workaround. |
| "Their spec sheet is the product" | Field reports beat brochures. Talk to people. |
| "Price is what matters" | Business model is what matters. Map how they earn. |
| "We can list them quickly" | This feeds pricing (13) and market validation (14). Depth pays. |

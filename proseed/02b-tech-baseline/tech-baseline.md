## Structural findings (technical)

- **Scale-up gap, not physics gap**: commercial microwave modules top out ~300 kW; a 50 t/h mineral line needs ~3–4 MW thermal (~10× array). Engineering, not invention — flagged in H9/H10.
- **Dielectric response varies**: iron ore heats well, silica poorly — kaolin/CaCO₃ response MUST be measured in Step 5a before H9/H10 economics hold.
- **ATEX is the recurring regulatory risk**: solvent atmospheres (H1) and flour dust (H6) both put the machine inside explosive-atmosphere compliance — design-cost driver.
- **Dielectric data scarcity is the software moat**: H3/H4/H5 all depend on temperature-dependent material property data that generalist tools lack — a build-or-buy strategic question for Step 4.

## Open technical questions for Step 5a (feasibility)

1. H9/H10: measure dielectric loss factor of kaolin and CaCO₃ at process temperatures.
2. H6: can MW + minimal moisture assist reach ≥90% lipase inactivation (vs ~85% MW-only)?
3. H7/H8: replicate Nottingham results on target ore bodies; cost per kWh/t at scale.
4. H1: ATEX-rated continuous microwave cavity feasibility.

## Evidence weighed

| Side | Evidence | Read |
|------|----------|------|

## DECISION: **GO — Develop + Trial**

10a proceeds **staged**, and 10b paid trials run in parallel as the commercial engine:

- **Tranche 1 released now:** M1 ore panel + M2 applicator ($950k) — capability risk retirement
- **Tranche 2 gated on trial signal:** M3–M5 (~$5M+) released only when 10b produces ≥1 paid trial converting to a site commitment, OR 2+ paying bench-test customers
- **Kill conditions** (written in advance, per gate honesty):
  - <50% of ore panel responds ≥10% BWi → kill or resegment
  - 12 months without a paying trial customer → kill
  - MicroHammer lands 2 production installs first → revisit positioning, don't auto-continue

## What would change the answer

A signed LOI from a large concentrator upgrades Tranche 2 to immediate release. A failed ore panel kills it regardless of traction.
